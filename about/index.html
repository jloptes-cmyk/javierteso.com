SCREENWRITER · DIRECTOR

JAVIER
L. TESO

Javier L. Teso (Barcelona, 1980) is a screenwriter and director whose work spans thriller, drama, fantasy and adventure. Since 2011, he has combined his career in education with screenwriting and filmmaking.

His early audiovisual work includes Noche Zombie and Hombre Lobo, created for Phenomena Experience Halloween screenings. He later directed the short films 228 (2013) and Natural Born Directors (2015), the latter made with support from Sant Boi de Llobregat City Council.

His current feature projects include The Executioner's Room, Back in Gear and Genpets. His screenplays have received awards at Liberty Films Festival, Your Way International Film Festival, Cinemabriz and the Los Angeles Film & Documentary Awards.

He is currently seeking production partners, representation and collaborators for his feature projects.

Barcelona, Spain
